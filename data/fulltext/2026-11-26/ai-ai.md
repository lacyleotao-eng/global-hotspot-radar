# Tidal to ban and remove millions of AI-generated tracks as AI slop inundates music streaming
[Ivan Mehta](https://techcrunch.com/author/ivan-mehta/)
8:46 AM PST · November 24, 2026
[ ](https://www.facebook.com/sharer.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F24%2Ftidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming%2F) [ ](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F24%2Ftidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming%2F&text=Tidal+to+ban+and+remove+millions+of+AI-generated+tracks+as+AI+slop+inundates+music+streaming&via=techcrunch) [ ](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F24%2Ftidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming%2F&title=Tidal+to+ban+and+remove+millions+of+AI-generated+tracks+as+AI+slop+inundates+music+streaming&summary=The+company+said+it+will+still+accept+songs+that+were+made+with+AI+assistance%2C+but+not+fully+generated.&mini=1&source=TechCrunch) [ ](https://www.reddit.com/submit?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F24%2Ftidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming%2F&title=Tidal+to+ban+and+remove+millions+of+AI-generated+tracks+as+AI+slop+inundates+music+streaming) [ ](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/tidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming/)
Music streaming service Tidal [said](https://x.com/TIDAL/status/2125233598714487170) on Tuesday that it will start banning and removing songs generated fully with AI in a bid to stem the flood of submissions to its platform. The company said that over the past 90 days, more than 43% of tracks uploaded to its platform were generated by AI, and that was a major reason driving this decision.
Tidal said it will start removing fully AI-generated music on December 9, and the process will result in more than 29 million songs being deleted from the platform by the end of the year. With the new rules in place, the platform said it will be rejecting roughly 80,000-85,000 tracks per day.
Tidal is also banning distribution services from delivering 100% AI-generated tracks, and will reject submissions if songs’ metadata says they are made fully using AI.
The company said it will still accept songs that were made with AI assistance, but not fully generated. 
The move comes as AI-generated music inundates streaming services across the board. Tidal is one of the first major platforms after [Bandcamp to ban AI music outright](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/14/bandcamp-takes-a-stand-against-ai-music-banning-it-from-the-platform/). Deezer, [which also provides detection tools to other streaming services](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/deezers-new-tool-can-identify-ai-music-from-spotify-apple-music-and-others/), said that it will introduce [a toggle to let users filter out AI music](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/14/deezer-will-soon-let-you-filter-out-ai-generated-music/). Larger platforms like [Spotify](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/spotify-will-label-ai-persona-profiles-and-exclude-their-music-from-recommendations/) and [YouTube Music](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/14/youtube-music-launches-new-labels-as-ai-generated-music-floods-streaming-platforms/) have adopted a milder approach to this issue, choosing to label AI-generated music instead of offering filters or taking stronger measures.
Tidal started [de-monetizing songs made using AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/tidal-cracks-down-on-ai-music-by-cutting-off-monetization/) in June, but that did not prove enough of a deterrent, Tidal Music’s lead, Robert Anderson, said on X.
> A few months ago, [@Tidal](https://x.com/TIDAL?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw) tagged and demonetized all wholly AI-generated tracks to protect the authenticity of real artists. But that’s enough, as 43% of tracks distributed to us in the last 90 days have been detected as AI-generated. Something more drastic needs to be done…
> — rsa (@rsa) [November 24, 2026](https://x.com/rsa/status/2125227948890563014?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)
The company said that 27 million, or 6.3% of tracks on the platform, are fully AI-generated, but 96% of those have no streams.
“It’s true that not all songs catch on immediately, and artists should have time to build their fanbases. But this situation is different – at this scale, the authentic music and artists that Tidal subscribers are looking to listen to and discover get buried,” the company [said in a post](https://x.com/TIDAL/status/2125233598714487170).
Meanwhile, music labels are trying to make money by partnering with [platforms](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/universal-music-group-and-tiktok-renew-agreement-to-combat-unauthorized-ai-music/) and [AI model makers](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/umg) to sell their catalog for training or remixing features. [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/pdfs/insights/goldman-sachs-research/music-in-the-air-10-years-on/report.pdf#:~:text=Market%20share%20dilution%20of%20royalty%20payouts%20as,to%20optimize%20catalog%20assets%20and%20improve%20discovery.) estimates that by 2035, AI-related licensing tools and new monetization opportunities have the potential to improve record labels’ revenue by 10%.
