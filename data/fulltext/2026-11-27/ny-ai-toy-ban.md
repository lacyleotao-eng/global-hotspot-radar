New York bans AI chatbot toys for kids.
Governor Kathy Hochul [signed a bill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gh0OPfn4-tg) putting a five-year pause on the manufacturing, distribution, and sale of AI chatbot toys, including “talking stuffed animals, dolls and robots that use the internet to hold conversations with kids.” This comes just one month after the state also [restricted](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1020112/ny-governor-kathy-hochul-signs-law-banning-harmful-ai-chatbot-features-for-kids) how chatbots could interact with minors.
[Governor Hochul Signs Five-Year Ban on AI Chatbot Toys](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/consumers-begin-their-holiday-shopping-season-governor-hochul-signs-five-year-ban-ai-chatbot)
