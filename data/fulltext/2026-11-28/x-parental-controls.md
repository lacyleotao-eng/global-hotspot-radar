# X finally gets parental controls after 20 years
[Ram Iyer](https://techcrunch.com/author/ram-iyer/)
11:31 AM PST · November 26, 2026
[ ](https://www.facebook.com/sharer.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F26%2Fx-finally-gets-parental-controls-after-20-years%2F) [ ](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F26%2Fx-finally-gets-parental-controls-after-20-years%2F&text=X+finally+gets+parental+controls+after+20+years&via=techcrunch) [ ](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F26%2Fx-finally-gets-parental-controls-after-20-years%2F&title=X+finally+gets+parental+controls+after+20+years&summary=Elon+Musk%27s+social+network+X+has+finally+added+parental+controls+for+teenagers%2C+giving+parents+and+guardians+a+way+to+manage+what+their+kids+see+on+the+platform.+&mini=1&source=TechCrunch) [ ](https://www.reddit.com/submit?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F11%2F26%2Fx-finally-gets-parental-controls-after-20-years%2F&title=X+finally+gets+parental+controls+after+20+years) [ ](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/x-finally-gets-parental-controls-after-20-years/)
After 20 years of existence, X, the social network formerly known as Twitter, has finally [added](https://x.com/Safety/status/2125709760066670625) parental controls for teenagers, giving parents and guardians a way to manage what their kids see and interact with on the platform. But unlike most platforms’ implementation of such controls, X is putting its own spin on the system by giving teens a say in the matter.
The company said accounts belonging to 13-to-17-year-olds will be set to “protected mode” by default, meaning they won’t be able to see sensitive content, and won’t be served targeted ads. However, X is allowing teenagers to retain a measure of control over their accounts, letting them see what restrictions were made to their accounts. Parents and guardians will also not be able to read their teens’ private messages, see search or viewing history, or post as their teen. 
The controls let parents see how much time their teen spends on X per week, set daily limits, require their approval for new followers, limit feed recommendations and videos to already followed accounts, filter specific words, turn off sensitive media, specify who can send their teen DMs, and see who they are messaging. 
The controls arrive months after X came under fire for a lack of controls preventing the platform’s AI chatbot from [being used to generate sexualized images of children and women](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/08/governments-grapple-with-the-flood-of-non-consensual-nudity-on-x/). The social network has also long been rife with [child sexual abuse material](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/27/among-the-worst-weve-seen-report-slams-xais-grok-over-child-safety-failures/), [misinformation](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/elon-musk-twitter-misinformation-timeline-1235076786/), AI bots, and openly lets users [post porn](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/03/x-tweaks-rules-to-formally-allow-adult-content/). 
X has generally not been a platform aimed at teenagers, but that doesn’t mean they don’t use the platform. A 2024 [study](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/19/a-surprising-number-of-ipad-kids-are-on-x-study-finds/) found that nearly a third of Gen Alpha kids have an X account. The platform itself requires users to be at least 13 years old, though that’s not a high bar given the kind of content on the platform. 
“Parents want practical tools, and teens deserve room to grow. With Parental Controls on X, families can set the guardrails that fit their home, while teens keep ownership of their accounts and can see every change. Striking that balance is vital and what we set out to build.” Michael O’Herlihy, head of safety at X, wrote in a blog post.
